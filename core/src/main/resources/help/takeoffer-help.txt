takeoffer

NAME
----
takeoffer - take an offer to buy or sell XMR

SYNOPSIS
--------
takeoffer
		--offer-id=<value>
		--payment-account-id=<value>
		--amount=<value>
		--challenge=<value>

DESCRIPTION
-----------
Take an existing offer using a payment account with a matching payment method.

OPTIONS
-------
--offer-id
		The id of the buy or sell offer to take.

--payment-account-id
		The id of the payment account used to send or receive funds during the trade.
		The payment account's payment method must match that of the offer.

--amount
		The optional amount of XMR to take from a range offer.  The default is the
		full offer amount.

--challenge
		The optional passphrase required to take a private offer.

EXAMPLES
--------
Take an offer:
$ ./haveno-cli --password=xyz --port=9998 takeoffer --offer-id=<offer-id> --payment-account-id=<payment-account-id>

Take 0.25 XMR of a range offer:
$ ./haveno-cli --password=xyz --port=9998 takeoffer --offer-id=<offer-id> --payment-account-id=<payment-account-id> --amount=0.25
