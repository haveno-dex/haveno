getmyoffers

NAME
----
getmyoffers - get your offers

SYNOPSIS
--------
getmyoffers
		--direction=<value>
		--currency-code=<value>

DESCRIPTION
-----------
Returns your offers with the given direction and currency-code.

OPTIONS
-------
--direction
		The offer direction (buy|sell).

--currency-code
		The three letter code of the currency traded for XMR, e.g., EUR, USD, BCH, ...

EXAMPLES
--------
Get your offers to sell XMR for USD:
$ ./haveno-cli --password=xyz --port=9998 getmyoffers --direction=sell --currency-code=usd
